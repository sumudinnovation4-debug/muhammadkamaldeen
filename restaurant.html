Our story

Cooked like home

“Iya Bisi cooked for forty people every Sunday and never once measured the pepper.”

Iya Bisi Kitchen started on a single gas burner in 2014 and grew one plate at a time. Today we still cook in small batches, grind our own pepper base each morning and smoke our own suya spice. Nothing here comes from a packet.

We buy peppers, tomatoes and leaves from Lagos markets and our fish from Lekki fishermen. We pay fairly, so the same people supply us year after year.
