Good to know

Questions

Can I try before I join?

Yes. A day pass is ₦5,000 and is deducted from your first month if you join the same week.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, for up to 30 days a year with a travel or medical note. Yearly members get 45 days.

Do I need to bring a towel?

No. Towels are included on every plan, plus lockers and showers.

Is there parking?

There is free parking for 60 cars and a security guard on the premises.

Do you have women-only hours?

Yes. Tuesday and Thursday from 10:00 to 12:00 the strength floor is women-only with a female coach.
